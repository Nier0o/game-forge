## Quick‑Start Visual Design Guide  
*(Everything can be made in a 16 × 16 or 32 × 32 pixel grid – the exact size is up to you.)*

---

### 1. Overall Mood & Color Palette

| Element | Color # | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Stone wall | #4A4A4A | Dark gray, slightly mottled |
| Rust / corrosion | #8B4C2A | Deep reddish‑brown |
| Dust / grime | #AFAFAF | Light gray, almost washed out |
| Leather | #5B3A1E | Brown‑ish, worn |
| Torch flame | #FF7F00 | Warm orange, no green glow |
| Fog | #C0C0C0 | Soft gray, semi‑transparent |
| Background (low‑light) | #2B2B2B | Very dark gray, not black |

- **Keep the palette tight** – 5–7 colors total.  
- Use **dithering** sparingly to blend stone and rust.  
- Avoid bright, saturated hues (no neon, no crystal blues).

---
