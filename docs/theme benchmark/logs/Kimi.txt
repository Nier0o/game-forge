You’re already designing—this *is* the brief.  
Here’s a visual ruleset you (or an artist) can follow without ever losing the feeling.

--------------------------------------------------
1. Palette (8-color master set, no others allowed)
   #0c0c0c almost-black  
   #23231c dirty brown-black  
   #3d3a33 cold stone  
   #50453a rotted wood  
   #6b5d52 old leather  
   #8b7f78 faded cloth  
   #a19c8a ashy highlight  
   #4a4e38 sick moss  

Use only these. No pure whites, no saturation above 15 %.  
Let single pixels of the moss color suggest lichen on walls; that’s the only “life” permitted.

--------------------------------------------------
2. Tiles (16×16 grid, 2-pixel outline inside edge so shapes read at 1× or 4×)

Wall  
Single-tone stone blocks, cracks 1 px wide, running like veins.  
One missing brick every 3-4 blocks; the gap is black inside = depth.

Floor  
Flat mid-gray, scattered 1–2 px speckles (dust).  
Occasional 8×8 stain darker than base; never repeated in same spot twice in a row.

Torch  
Stick: 3 vertical pixels wide, 6 high, brown-black.  
Flame: 5 px teardrop, dithered 50 % transparency of ash highlight color.  
Light halo: 32 px radius gradient using only the three darkest colors; no circles, jagged edge to feel smoky.

--------------------------------------------------
3. Character template (24×32 sprite, 3-frame walk)

Silhouette first: hood blob, slumped shoulders, no neck.  
One leather strap across chest, 1 px thick.  
Feet are 2×2 rectangles; knees barely lift.  
No eyes visible under hood; if facing player, two single-pixel dots of highlight color max.  
Animation: head stays down, body shifts 1 px left/right; the exhaustion is in the lack of bob.

--------------------------------------------------
4. Items (8×8 or 16×16, placed on ground)

Key  
Three teeth only, blunt, gray same as stone.  
1 px highlight on edge to catch torchlight.

Potion  
Crude clay bottle, 8 px high, neck thicker than modern vials.  
Cork is just 2 brown pixels.  
No label; one 1-pixel crack near base.

Weapon  
Short sword: blade 12 px, chipped edge drawn with single missing pixel every 4 px.  
Handle wrapped in leather color, two wraps only, frayed end sticking out 1 px.

--------------------------------------------------
5. Fog / Depth  
Overlay a 64×64 semi-transparent tile (use color #0c0c0c at 30 % opacity) scrolling slowly.  
Let it erase detail; the player should squint, not admire.

--------------------------------------------------
6. Story without text  
- Doorframe taller than walls = once important, now empty hinges.  
- Blood replaced by 2-pixel darker stain; no red, just shade.  
- Chains hanging from ceiling, last link broken open = escape, not rescue.  
- Footprints in dust: 1 px dots, stride longer than player’s = someone hurried, still out there.

--------------------------------------------------
7. Technical limits to keep it honest  
Max sprite size 32×32.  
Max animation frames 3 for anything that moves.  
No shader, no rotation; flip horizontal only.  
Light is painted into sprites, not engine light.  
Screen shake 1 px left/right for hits, 2 frames only.

--------------------------------------------------
8. One-sentence reminder to yourself  
“If I want to add something, first remove something else.”
